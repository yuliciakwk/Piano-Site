About Jasmine

An accomplished pianist, Jasmine began playing at the age of four. By nine, she had already reached performer level, passing exams set by the Associated Board of the Royal Schools of Music (ABRSM) and Trinity College London.

At 13, while most of her peers were still focused on school, Jasmine was both studying and teaching piano. From 16 to 19, she travelled to England for three months each year to complete her piano program and graduate from the music academy. She later continued her studies at the Hong Kong Music Institute, including training in conducting.

Frequently Asked Questions

What age can my child start lessons?

Children can begin lessons from as young as 3 years old. Lessons for young beginners are adapted to their age and attention span, building a strong foundation from the very start.

Do you offer online lessons?

Yes. Jasmine has taught online since 2020 and continues to offer virtual lessons in both piano playing and music theory over Zoom, Google Meet or WhatsApp video call.

Do I need a piano at home?

Yes, regular practice at home is essential for steady progress. An acoustic piano is ideal, but a digital piano with 88 full-sized, weighted keys is a great option for beginners. Feel free to ask for advice on choosing an instrument.

Do you prepare students for exams?

Absolutely, exam preparation is a core part of Jasmine's teaching. She prepares students for Royal Conservatory of Music (RCM) examinations at every level, from the early grades all the way to the advanced ARCT diploma.
