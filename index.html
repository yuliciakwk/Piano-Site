About Jasmine

An accomplished pianist, Jasmine began playing at the age of four. By nine, she had already reached performer level, passing exams set by the Associated Board of the Royal Schools of Music (ABRSM) and Trinity College London.

At 13, while most of her peers were still focused on school, Jasmine was both studying and teaching piano. From 16 to 19, she travelled to England for three months each year to complete her piano program and graduate from the music academy. She later continued her studies at the Hong Kong Music Institute, including training in conducting.
